How Someday works
Anything can be parked here: a task, a project with its tasks, a goal, or an idea from a note, chat or digest. It leaves Today, Tasks and the Open counts, keeps its original creation date, and records the day it was parked. Three months later it comes up in Reset for a decision: Bring back (pick a date, project or cycle), Keep for three more months, or Purge (to the trash, 30 days to undo).
