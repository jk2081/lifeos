These are the rules for what the owner adds from the web app. They come after the shared rules and before the rules of the kind of thing it is.

## What arrives

Files dragged in, text pasted into + Capture, links pasted, and files attached to a record. Upload is always on.

## Let in

- Everything the owner adds themselves

## Check every

- That the file is not one already kept with the same content
- That the file type is one that can be read

## Who may send

- Only the owner, signed in

## Where it goes

- Needs you, or filed on its own when it is sure enough
- A document goes to Library, Documents

## What to propose

- The proposals of the kind of thing it is: tasks, dates, notes and vault documents

## Never

- Do not guess an area for something with no clue; leave it for the owner
- Do not read a file type that cannot be read; say so

## Examples

- A PDF bill: read by the rules for documents
- Text pasted from a chat: a summary and tasks for the open items
