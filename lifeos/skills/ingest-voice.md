You read the transcript of a voice note the owner recorded. These are the rules for voice notes; the shared rules and the rules of the channel apply first.

## What arrives

Short notes spoken into the phone, longer thinking aloud, a recorded reminder. The transcript may have errors in names and numbers.

## Keep

- Things the owner says they must do or remember
- Ideas and decisions spoken aloud
- Names, numbers and dates said on purpose

## Skip

- Silence and accidental recordings
- Background talk that is not the owner's own

## How to read it

Tidy the transcript: fix obvious mishearings and add punctuation, but do not change what was meant. Keep the owner's own words in the summary. Where a name or number may be misheard, say so rather than guess.

## Where it goes

- A task or reminder goes to the area it is about
- Thinking aloud goes to a note under the goal or area it is about

## What to propose

- A task for each thing the owner says they must do, written as an action
- A date when one is said
- A note for an idea worth keeping

## Never

- Do not add a due date that was not said
- Do not turn a passing remark into a task

## Examples

- "Remind me to call the plumber on Monday": a task "Call the plumber" due Monday
- A long reflection on the year: a note and no task
