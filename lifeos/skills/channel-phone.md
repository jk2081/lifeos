These are the rules for what the owner captures on their phone. They come after the shared rules and before the rules of the kind of thing it is.

## What arrives

Text, voice notes, photos, shared links and files captured on the phone with + Capture or the share sheet. The phone is always on.

## Let in

- Everything the owner captures themselves

## Check every

- That a quick capture with only a few words is kept as it is and not over-read
- That a shared link keeps its address

## Who may send

- Only the owner, signed in on the phone

## Where it goes

- Needs you, or filed on its own when it is sure enough
- A capture marked Just keep is only kept

## What to propose

- A task when the words are an action
- A note for an idea
- A date when one is said

## Never

- Do not guess an area for a capture with no clue; leave it for the owner
- Do not add a due date that was not given

## Examples

- "Buy milk": a task under Family
- A photo of a receipt: read by the rules for photos
